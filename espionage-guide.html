← Espionage

The idea

Everyone is an 🛡️ Agent, except a few 🕶️ Spies hiding amongst them, led by the Black Hat. Your phone is your badge. Scanning someone's badge uses your role's ability, and what you learn arrives at the briefing. Then you talk, and vote to detain someone.

The Agents win when they detain the Black Hat. The Spies win when there are as many Spies still online as Agents.

4 to 12 players, in one room. One TV or laptop that everyone can see, and a phone each.

Setting up

  1. On the TV or laptop, open Game Night → Espionage → Host on the TV and start a new game.
  2. Everyone scans the QR code on the screen with their phone and types their name.
  3. When everyone's in, the host chooses the times and deals the roles. Look at yours, then hide it.

Each round

  1. 🤝 Mingle (5 minutes). Walk around. You have 2 scans: point your phone at someone's badge, or type the code under it. Choose your ability or a plain 📶 ping. Use at least one scan, or the briefing names you. Badges change every 30 seconds, so you can't scan a photo.
  2. 📋 Briefing. The screen shows who the Black Hat took offline, if a Firewall blocked an attack, and who didn't scan. Your phone shows your reports, and maybe a 📨 message from HQ.
  3. 🗣️ Council (3 minutes). Share what you learned, or lie about it.
  4. 🗳️ Vote (1 minute), on your phone. The most votes is detained; a tie detains nobody. The screen reveals the votes one by one. A detained player's team stays secret: if the game goes on, they weren't the Black Hat.

Offline and detained players can still talk and ask questions, but can't scan or vote. Keep your badge showing: Forensics may want to scan it.
