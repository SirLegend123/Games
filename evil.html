A secret grimoire for the evil team in Blood on the Clocktower

Evil Grimoire

You've woken up evil. This keeps your team, your bluffs and your lies straight, and helps you pick who to kill, who to poison and who to push to the block.

⌂ All apps

  1. Set up your team

    Tap New game and type the players' names in seat order, going clockwise.

    Then, under Your team on the Table & team tab:

    • Pick your real character and tap your seat.
    • Tap your teammates, and their characters if you know them.
    • Choose the Demon bluffs: good characters that aren't in play.

    The app shows how many evil players there should be, so you know when your whole team is marked.

  2. Build your story

    On Story & night, choose the good character you'll claim. A Demon bluff is safest: nobody can truthfully counter-claim it.

    • Each night, tap Safe, Cover a teammate or Frame a threat to get made-up info for your bluff.
    • Save it to What you've said so you can repeat it word for word.
    • Story checks warn you about slips, like first-night-only info on night 3.

  3. During the day

    Tap a player on the circle to note their claims: Soft for every role they offered, Hard for the one they commit to. Mark who on your team they suspect, and whether the town trusts them.

    • After each chat, tap I spoke to someone on the Today tab: their claims, their info, and what you told them.
    • Log public statements and each nomination with its votes in the Town square.
    • Record each Virgin nomination and Slayer shot from under the circle.
    • Today also shows who to push for execution, who not to nominate, and your team's votes.

  4. Night, and the end of the game

    • End day → night in the middle of the circle asks who was executed and whether the game ended.
    • At night, Story & night ranks who to kill, poison or protect. Tick your choice and tap Confirm.
    • Night → day asks who actually died. A Soldier, Monk or Mayor can stop a kill, so it's never assumed.
    • If your Demon dies, a Scarlet Woman takeover or a star-pass is handled for you.

    When the game ends, the Game recap shows how your team did: deaths, kills that landed, slips and a timeline.

Tap Hide at the top whenever someone looks over your shoulder; double-tap the screen to come back. Everything stays on this device. Blood on the Clocktower belongs to The Pandemonium Institute; this is an unofficial fan tool. Good players: the Clocktower Notebook is for you.
