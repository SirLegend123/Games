Privacy notice

These apps are a free, unofficial fan project for playing Blood on the Clocktower. There are no adverts, no tracking, no analytics, and nothing is sold. This notice explains what is kept about you, who can see it, and for how long.

Last updated 13 November 2026.

🕰️ Clocktower Daily and 🗝️ The Shadows

Clocktower Daily and The Shadows store each game online, so that everyone can play on their own phone over several days. Everything below applies to both. (In The Shadows, your "character" is your side, Guest or Shadow; the Shadows' own chat is seen only by the Shadows and the host; your vote at the round table is secret until voting closes; and wherever this notice says "the Storyteller", read "the host".)

What is kept

An anonymous sign-inWhen you open the app, it signs in with a random ID. No email address, password or phone number is ever asked for.
Your nameThe name you type when you join (it doesn't have to be your real name).
What you do in the gameYour seat and character, the information the Storyteller gives you, your night choices, your nominations and their reasons, your defences, your votes, and your messages: in the Town Square, in private chats with other players, and with the Storyteller.
When you were last in the appSo the Storyteller can see who's keeping up, and send a reminder.
Notifications (only if you turn them on)The address your phone's notification service gives the app to reach your phone, and the name of your browser and phone type (for example "iPhone, Safari"), to help fix problems.

Who can see it

  • Everyone in the game (anyone with the game code) sees the names in the town, who is dead, the Town Square, the nominations with their reasons and defences, and, once voting has closed, who voted for whom.
  • Your character and private information are seen only by you and the Storyteller until the game ends. At the end, everyone sees the whole Grimoire: every character.
  • Private chats between two players are seen only by those two players. The Storyteller can't read them.
  • The Shadows' daily phrases: on days with a code phrase or the Whisper, the server automatically checks private messages, and Guests' messages to the host, for that day's phrases or words. Nobody reads the messages: the host only learns who said a phrase, to whom and when, and whether a Guest caught the code phrase.
  • Your chat with the Storyteller is seen only by you and the Storyteller.
  • The Storyteller also sees everyone's night choices and when each player was last in the app.

Where it's kept

  • Games are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Authentication), in Google's London data centre. Notifications are sent by a small program that also runs there.
  • Notifications travel through your phone's own notification service (Apple, Google or Mozilla). They are encrypted on the way, so those services can't read them.
  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the lettering comes from Google Fonts. Like any website, these see your device's internet address when your browser loads the page.

How long it's kept

  • A game, with everything in it, is deleted automatically 30 days after it ends.
  • A game that never starts is deleted 30 days after it was set up, and one left unfinished 90 days after it started.
  • The Storyteller can delete a game at any time (Settings, then Delete this game). That removes it for everyone, straight away.
  • If you turn notifications off (under Me), your notification address is deleted straight away.

Why it's kept

Only to run the game you chose to join, and to send you the notifications you asked for. Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR), this is done because of a legitimate interest in running the game for its players, and, for notifications, with your consent, which you can take back at any time by turning them off.

📱 On your phone

The apps remember a few things on your own device, using your browser's storage: which game you're in, your sign-in ID, light or dark mode, which messages you've read, and your notes. None of it is used for tracking. Clearing your browser's data for this website removes it.

🎭 The other apps

  • Storyteller's Grimoire, Player's Grimoire and Town Square link devices at the same table directly, phone to phone. A free connection service (PeerJS) helps the devices find each other, and sees their internet addresses and a random ID. If a direct link isn't possible, the traffic is passed on, encrypted, through a relay server (PeerJS or Open Relay). Nothing about the game is stored online: it lives on the Storyteller's device.
  • Online Notes and Table Notes are kept only on your own device. They're never sent anywhere.

⚖️ Your rights

You can ask to see what's kept about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted, and you can object to it being used. The quickest way is to ask your Storyteller: they can remove you from a game, or delete the whole game. In Clocktower Daily you can message them under Chats.

You can also contact the person who made these apps through the project's GitHub page, github.com/SirLegend123/Games. Messages there are public, so don't put personal details in them: just ask for a private way to get in touch.

If you're unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk.

Children

These apps are not meant for children under 13.
