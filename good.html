Clocktower Notebook

Keep track of who claims what, what everyone learned at night, and who might be evil. Everything you enter is saved automatically and stays private to you.

⌂ All apps

  1. Set up the table

    Tap New game, choose the script, and type the players' names in seat order, going clockwise.

    The game opens on Night 1. Pick your seat and your character, then log what you learned. Your character becomes your claim on the circle.

  2. During the day

    Tap a player's token to open their panel: what to ask them, what they told you, and under More about them, alive or dead, your read and notes.

    • Soft claim (the default): tap every role they offered, like a 3-for-3 or 4-for-4, in any order.
    • Hard claim: when they firmly commit to one role, switch to Hard claim and tap it. That role is then removed from other players' options.
    • Set My read (good, unsure, evil), pick the evil character you suspect, and write notes.

    After talking to someone, tap their token, or Talk on their row in Who's who. Their panel starts with the questions to ask them, each with answer buttons where the answer is definite: their options to pick from, Answer as the Fortune Teller (and so on) to enter their night info under the question, or Yes / No. Answers go straight into your notebook. Below that, record their claim, then pick from Which character's info? to enter the night info they shared (only that form opens). On the same page, record their soft or hard claim, the night info they shared, and anything they heard: another player's claim, or info from a character ("the Investigator saw a Baron"). It all goes onto the circle and into Night info for you.

    When a player shares what they learned at night, add it under Night info with Whose info set to them. If you hear info but don't know who it came from yet (say, "the Investigator saw a Baron"), set Whose info to Someone (not sure who yet). It links to them when they hard-claim that role. Use Slayer shot when someone shoots.

  3. End the day

    Tap End day → night in the middle of the circle. The app asks, one step at a time:

    • Who was executed, and whether it was the Virgin.
    • Whether the game ended.
    • Your night info, or that you died in the night.
    • Who died overnight.

  4. Read the board

    Use the tabs at the top: Table (the circle and the story so far), Night info, Plan, Evil team, Claims and Notes. Badges show when something needs attention.

    • Who's who (under the circle): a row for every player, most suspicious first. It shows what they've shared each day, how suspicious they are and why (or that they're confirmed or probably good), whether you've spoken to them today, and a Talk button. In their panel, answer yes/no questions with Yes, No or Didn't answer (not answering is never held against anyone), and enter their night info with quick answers, which go straight into Night info.
    • The story so far (beside the circle): your own nights (and tonight's suggested choice), what adds up and what doesn't, and what each player has told you and others. A recap of the day opens as night falls, and you can reread it during the night.
    • Plan for tomorrow: what doesn't add up, questions to ask each player, and who to look at.
    • Evil team: the most likely Demon and why, plus which Minions are likely in play.
    • Test a hunch: in a player's panel, tap "What if they're evil?" to see what supports it, what goes against it, and what follows if it's true.
    • Night info grades each player's information as consistent, doubtful or unreliable.
    • Changed claims: if a player gives info as one role and later claims another, or swaps a hard claim, the app flags it on the Plan tab, rings them amber and stops trusting that info. A real typo can be cleared with Forget this in their panel.
    • Game wrap-up: when the game ends, enter who was evil, then everyone's real character (or skip that), to see how the evidence held up, which information was right, whose claims were true, how your reads did, and a timeline.
    • Claim matrix: every claim at a glance. Amber means two players claim the same role.

Reading a token

Blue ring: you think they're good
Red ring: you think they're evil
Dashed amber ring: the app finds them suspicious from claims and info
✓ GOODProven good by the Virgin or a Slayer shot
Black shroud: dead
White dot: ghost vote still unused
Imp?The evil character you suspect

The suggestions are prompts, not answers. Anyone can be drunk or poisoned, and evil players lie. Blood on the Clocktower belongs to The Pandemonium Institute; this is an unofficial fan tool.
