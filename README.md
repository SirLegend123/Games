# Games

Open `index.html` to choose between the *Blood on the Clocktower* apps: the **Storyteller**, the **Town Square**, the **Player** and **Online Notes**. They share their character list (`characters.js`).

**Updates**: browsers keep a copy of each app for a while, so a device can still be on an old version after an update. Every app checks the site every couple of minutes (and whenever it's brought back to the screen), and when there's a newer version it shows **🔄 A new version is ready. Tap to update**; the game is kept. A phone linked to a Storyteller app that's out of date says so, and works out the night by itself. (When changing the apps, raise the number in `version.json` and `window.APP_V` in each page together.)

## Storyteller: face to face

**＋ New game** at the top starts again at any point (asking first if a game is under way), keeping the players. **🏠** goes back to the cover page; the game is saved, so opening the Storyteller again carries on where you left off and the phones and TV reconnect.

**Playing like a Jackbox game**: every game gets a 5-letter code as soon as it's created. Put the **Town Square** on a TV and it shows a QR code, the web address and the code; players scan it (or open the Player app and type the code) on their phones and tap their name, and each one lights up on the TV as they join. The Storyteller's Players and Bag screens show the code and who has joined (📱). When the bag is ready, **📱 Send tokens to phones** deals the characters at random and sends each player theirs, privately, to their own phone, which opens their Grimoire. Anyone without a phone gets their token by passing the Storyteller's phone round, as before. **＋ New game** clears everyone's phone for the next game.

A *Blood on the Clocktower* Storyteller app for games round a real table. Open `storyteller.html` (or `index.html`) in any browser; nothing to install. The game is saved on the device as you go.

1. **Players**: choose the script (Trouble Brewing, Bad Moon Rising or Sects & Violets) and add everyone in seat order, clockwise. Type names one at a time or several separated by commas. Move or remove players with the arrows and ×. Shows how many Townsfolk, Outsiders, Minions and Demon 5 to 15 players need.
2. **Bag**: tap characters to put them in the bag, or roll a 🎲 random setup and adjust it. The counts turn amber when they don't match, allowing for the Baron, Godfather, Fang Gu and Vigormortis. Choose what the Drunk (a Townsfolk not in play) or Lunatic (a Demon) will see.
3. **Draw**: pass the phone round. Each player taps their name, picks one of the face-down tokens and sees their character, good or evil, and ability, then hides it before passing on. Someone not at the table can draw later, and the Storyteller can stop the draw. The screen stays on, and a reload carries on without showing the last token.
4. **Grimoire**: once everyone has drawn, the phone goes back to the Storyteller and the game moves to the Grimoire circle: every seat with its character, symbol, name and reminders (good in blue, evil in red, the Drunk's or Lunatic's fake character underneath). Tap a player to change their character, good or evil, alive or dead, add or remove reminders, or **Show them their character** again.
   - **Demon bluffs**: 3 good characters not in play, picked for you (🎲 Pick for me to reroll) or chosen from the lists. They show in the middle of the circle.
   - **Evil team**: whether the Minions and Demon learn who each other are on Night 1: **Always** (the default) or **Only with 7+ players**, as in the official rules. When they do, each evil player's phone fills in their evil Grimoire by itself: teammates marked, the Demon's bluffs filled in.
   - **Red herring**: picked for you when there's a Fortune Teller (or a Drunk who thinks they are one), and can be changed.
5. **Nights**: a step-by-step guide through everyone who wakes, in wake order (the first night's order on Night 1, the other nights' order after that). For each step the Storyteller sees who to wake (highlighted on the circle), a warning if their info can be false (Drunk, poisoned, next to the No Dashii, Vortox), and the info the app has worked out, which can be switched between true and false or changed by hand. Then **Show them** turns the screen into what that player sees:
   - Minion and Demon info (7 or more players): the Minions see the Demon and each other; the Demon sees their Minions, the 3 bluffs and, in Bad Moon Rising, the Lunatic. The Lunatic sees fake Minions and the bluffs as if they were the Demon.
   - Info characters see their info: the Washerwoman, Librarian and Investigator their 2 players and character, the Chef, Empath, Clockmaker and Mathematician a number, the Godfather the Outsiders in play, the Grandmother their grandchild, the Evil Twin and their twin each other, and the Spy the whole Grimoire.
   - Choosing characters (Poisoner, Fortune Teller, Butler, Witch and the rest, on all three scripts) tap their players or character on the phone and get their answer straight away: the Fortune Teller's Yes or No (with the red herring), the Dreamer's two characters, the Seamstress's same or different, the Chambermaid's number. A Snake Charmer who picks the Demon swaps there and then.
   - Choices leave reminders on the circle (Poisoned, Master, Cursed, Mad, Safe, Drunk, Grandchild, Twin), and a poisoned player's later info is flagged. The Drunk wakes as the character they think they are.
   - When a player is done (Close your eyes, Not tonight, or the Storyteller's go back link), the phone shows a cover: **Close your eyes, pass the phone back to the Storyteller**. Only **I'm the Storyteller** takes it back to the Grimoire, so a player never sees it by accident. **Show them again** reopens their screen. Show them their character on the Grimoire works the same way.
   - **📱 On the player's phone**: when the woken player has joined on their phone, **📱 Send to their phone** sends them the step instead of handing over this phone. Their phone buzzes and shows exactly what this phone would (their info, or their choice, in their team's colour), they tap their choice there, and it comes straight back. The Minions' info goes to every Minion's phone at once (tap **Done** when they've seen it). **Take it back** returns to showing it on this phone. Check or change the info before sending, as usual.
   - **Back** undoes a step; **Skip** passes over someone. At dawn there's a log of the whole night, then **Start Day 1**.
   - **From Night 2**: Demons choose who to kill on the phone (the Imp, Zombuul when nobody died today, Pukka, Shabaloth's 2, Po's 0 or 3, Fang Gu, No Dashii, Vortox, Vigormortis), along with the Godfather and Assassin. After the player closes their eyes, the Storyteller sees who was hit and whether they die, with the reason they might not: the Monk's or Innkeeper's protection, the Soldier, the Tea Lady, the Sailor, the Fool, or a drunk or poisoned Demon. Each can be switched between Dies and Survives, and the Mayor can bounce the kill to someone else.
   - **Changed characters**: an Imp who kills themselves passes to a Minion (the Scarlet Woman first), a Fang Gu jumps to an Outsider, the Snake Charmer swaps, the Pit-Hag transforms, and a Scarlet Woman takes over when the Demon is executed with 5 or more alive. Each wakes on the next step (or night) to see **Your character has changed**.
   - **Night info**: the Ravenkeeper wakes if they died tonight, the Undertaker if someone was executed today, and the Sage if the Demon killed them. The Empath, Fortune Teller, Flowergirl, Town Crier, Oracle, Juggler (Night 2), Dreamer, Chambermaid and Mathematician get their info as on Night 1. The Gossip and Tinker are Storyteller-only steps.
   - Once-per-game abilities (Philosopher, Courtier, Seamstress, Professor, Assassin) stop waking once used. Reminders that last a day (Poisoned, Protected, Safe, Cursed, Mad, Master) clear at dusk, and a drunk or poisoned player's choice has no effect.
   - **Team colours**: a woken player's screen is tinted with their team's colour: red for evil (the Minions, the Demon, and the Lunatic who thinks they're the Demon), blue for Townsfolk (and the Drunk, who thinks they are one) and light blue for Outsiders. Every character shown to a player has a border in its team's colour too: red for Minions and Demons, blue for Townsfolk, light blue for Outsiders, as does the token they draw.
   - **Big text**: everything a woken player reads (names, choices, numbers, characters, buttons) is large enough to read across a table.
   - **The Demon's own team**: on a Demon's kill screen, the Minions they were shown on Night 1 (7 or more players) are marked **Minion** in red, and their own name **You**, so they don't kill their team by mistake. A Lunatic sees their fake Minions marked the same way.
   - **Dawn reveal**: Start Day first shows the whole table a red dawn screen with the dead players' names huge across it: who was found dead in the night (names only, no characters), or that nobody died.
   - At dawn the app announces who died, says if good or evil has won, and keeps the night in **The game so far**.
6. **Days**:
   - **Town view**: by default the day shows a circle with only names, who's dead and who still has a ghost vote, so it's safe if players see the screen. **👁 Show the Grimoire** switches to the full Grimoire (tap a player to change them) and **🙈 Hide** switches back.
   - **Three parts to the day**, shown along the top of the day screen, each with its own timer: **💬 Private chats** (5 min), **🗣️ Public discussion** (3 min) and **📢 Nominations** (5 min). Change the length of each with the Length menu (it's remembered for later games). The first timer starts when you tap ▶ Start the timer; moving on to the next part starts its timer straight away. You can go back to an earlier part until the first nomination starts; starting a nomination moves the day on to Nominations.
   - **📣 Said to everyone**: what players have posted to the town from their phones, with × to take a message down.
   - **⏱ Full screen**: a full-screen countdown for discussion, readable across the room. Pick 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 or 10 minutes, then Start / Pause, −1 / +1 minute and Reset. It turns amber in the last 30 seconds; at zero it flashes red, says Time's up! and beeps and buzzes where the device allows. Close keeps it running, with the time left shown on the Timer button. The screen stays on while it's open.
   - **✋ From the phones**: players linked on their phones get a hand bar at the bottom of the screen during the day. **✋ Nominate someone** lets them pick who to nominate; their raised hand appears on the TV and here ("Sophie wants to nominate Dom") with **Start** and ×, checked against the day's rules. When a vote is open, every phone that can vote shows **✋ Raise my hand** (warning when it uses a ghost vote); hands go up and down live here and on the TV, with the Butler rule applied. Hands can still be tapped here for anyone without a phone.
   - **Nominations**: tap who is nominating (alive players who haven't nominated today), then who they nominate (anyone not yet nominated today). Tap everyone who raises a hand; dead players spend their ghost vote. The app shows how many votes are needed (half the living, rounded up), who is on the block, and ties (nobody on the block). The Butler rule is enforced: while the Butler is alive and not drunk or poisoned, their button is locked (🔒 after their master's name, without saying why) until their master has voted, and if the master takes their vote back, the Butler's goes too. A drunk, poisoned or dead Butler votes freely. Delete a nomination with ×.
   - **Virgin**: the first time the Virgin is nominated, the app checks whether the nominator is a Townsfolk (noting the Spy and the Drunk) and whether the Virgin is drunk or poisoned, and suggests what happens. **Execute** kills the nominator and ends the day; **Nothing happens** goes on to the vote. Either way the Virgin's ability is used up.
   - **🏹 Slayer shot**: tap who shoots (anyone can claim to be the Slayer) and who they shoot. The app says what happens: nothing unless the shooter really is the Slayer, hasn't used their ability and isn't drunk or poisoned, and the target is the Demon (the Recluse can register as one, your call). The Demon dies on a hit, and the Scarlet Woman takes over with 5 or more alive. The real Slayer's ability is used up either way. × undoes a shot.
   - **End the day**: execute whoever is on the block, or nobody. It warns when the Devil's Advocate, Pacifist, Fool or Zombuul might stop the death, and offers **Executed, but they don't die**.
   - **Game over**: the game stops on a **Good wins!** or **Evil wins!** screen, with the reason and the whole Grimoire to reveal, as soon as the Demon is dead (executed, shot or killed at night, with no Scarlet Woman to take over or Mastermind to play on), the Saint or the Evil Twin's good twin is executed, only 2 players are left, nobody is executed while the Vortox lives, or the Mayor survives a day of 3 with no execution. **📜 Read the story** gives the Storyteller's script to read out at the reveal, a paragraph per night and day. It follows the good team: what they did and learnt, who was found dead, and Demon attacks the Monk, Soldier or Tea Lady stopped. Each player's name and character are revealed together only when they die ("Roman was found dead. Roman was the Fortune Teller."); hidden evil players are just "a player", and the Drunk is called by the character they believed they were. When an evil player is unmasked, a flashback tells what they'd been up to ("Looking back, Dad had been busy: they poisoned Sophie on Night 1 and Mum on Night 2."). Repeats are skipped (a Butler keeping the same master, info that hasn't changed) and similar deeds are merged. It ends with who won, the rest of the good team, then the evil team with their flashbacks, saving the Demon for last. **Copy as text** copies it. **Undo: carry on playing** goes back if you made a mistake; **New game** starts again with the same players. Otherwise **Reopen the day** undoes the execution, and **Start Night N** carries on.

## Player's Grimoire

A *Blood on the Clocktower* app for each player at a real table. Open `player.html`. Everything is saved on the device.

**Welcome**: the app opens on a welcome page: scan the QR code on the TV or type the Storyteller's code, then tap your name. You then wait on a **Welcome, (name)** screen, which counts how many players have joined, until the Storyteller sends the tokens; your character arrives and your Grimoire opens. No code? **Set up the players myself** goes to the steps below.

**🌙 Night and 🌅 dawn**: while linked, at night the phone shows a night screen: close your eyes, the Storyteller will wake you if you need to act, with 💬 buttons to quietly message the neighbours either side of you. **📖 My Grimoire** peeks at your notes (a 🌙 button at the top goes back). When the Storyteller wakes you, your night action comes up over it. (The Spy's phone shows the Grimoire as a list: each player's character, side, what a Drunk or Lunatic thinks they are, the reminders, and the Demon's bluffs.) In the morning the phone shows who was found dead (or that nobody died), the same as the TV, and tells you if it's you. It goes away when you tap Continue or the Storyteller closes the dawn news.

**☀️ Light / 🌙 Dark** at the top switches between light and dark mode. The phone's own setting is used until you choose, and your choice is remembered.

**📜 Characters** at the top, on every screen, opens the script's characters and what they do, grouped by team, with how many of each team there are for this many players. Your own character is marked **You**. Close it with Close, Esc or by tapping outside.

1. **Players**: scan the QR code on the Town Square, or type the Storyteller's code in **🔗 Join the game** and tap which player is you: the script and the players fill in by themselves, in seat order, and from then on deaths and the night and day follow the Storyteller's game (your own notes, claims and reads are kept). Otherwise, choose the script and add everyone in seat order, clockwise, including yourself (several at once with commas). Tap **Me** next to your own name.
2. **My token**: when the Storyteller sends the tokens, yours arrives on your phone: **Your token has arrived** asks you to make sure nobody can see, then **Show me** reveals your character, its ability and your side, and **Open my Grimoire** takes you in (good or evil, set for you). If you're not linked, or were handed a physical token, then once the Storyteller hands you your character, tap it (its ability is shown), then choose your side: **Good** or **Evil**. You can come back and change either later.
3. **Grimoire** (good players):
   - **The circle**: every player in their seat, showing the character they claim (with its symbol), your read of them (blue for trust, grey for unsure, red for suspect, a darker red and a **Demon?** badge for your Demon guess), whether they're dead and whether they still have their ghost vote. You're marked ★ with your own character. The middle shows the phase, how many are alive and the votes needed to execute.
   - **Tap a player** to record what they claim (with that character's ability), set your read, mark them dead or their ghost vote used, and keep notes on what they've said.
   - **Phase**: ‹ › steps through Night 1, Day 1, Night 2…
   - **My info**: your character and ability, and a log of what you've learnt, filed under the current night or day.
   - **Claims**: everyone's claim in one list, with double claims flagged, and how many Outsiders there should be for the number of players.
   - **New game** keeps the players and clears the rest.
4. **Grimoire** (evil players):
   - **The circle**: your teammates in red with their real characters (and an "as …" tag for their bluff), everyone else with what they claim and how dangerous you rate them (Harmless, Watch, Threat or 🎯 Target). You're shown with your real character and your bluff.
   - **Tap a player** to mark them as on your team (and their real character), set their claim or bluff, rate how dangerous they are, mark deaths and ghost votes, and keep notes.
   - **My team**: you and your teammates with their characters and bluffs.
   - **Bluffs**: the Demon's three bluffs, each marked free, used by your team or claimed by the town; the character you're claiming, with its ability and a warning if anyone else claims it; and a warning if two of your team bluff as the same character.
   - **Who to kill** (Demons): living players not on your team, most dangerous first.
   - **My cover story**: a log by night and day of what really happened and, kept apart, what you'll say (🗣️), so your story stays the same.
   - **What the town claims**: every good player's claim, with double claims and claims of your bluffs flagged.

## Online Notes

A notepad for playing the official online game. Open `notes.html` (or **Online Notes** on the cover page). Nothing links to the other apps; everything is saved on the device.

1. **Players**: choose the script (or **Another script**, where you type character names, with suggestions) and add everyone in seat order. Tap **Me** next to your name.
2. **My character**: tap your character.
3. **Notes**: the town in a circle, with **‹ Night 1 ›** to move through the nights and days.
   - **My info**: what you learn, noted against the night or day it is.
   - Tap a player to add a **soft claim** (a hint or a maybe) or a **hard claim** (they've said outright): tap the character's button, add what they said if you like, then **Add claim**. Their token shows their latest hard claim (gold) or, failing that, soft claim (dashed). A **!** flags a story that has changed, two players hard claiming the same character, or someone claiming your character.
   - **Died?**: tap how they died (executed, died at night, Slayer shot, Virgin or other). It's noted for the current night or day, and the token greys out with a 👻 until you mark their ghost vote used.
   - Notes for each player, **The town** (everyone's claim and fate at a glance) and **Deaths** in order.
   - **New game** keeps the players.

## Town Square

A live public view of the Storyteller's game for a TV, projector or spare tablet. Open `town.html`.

- **Linking up**: on the Storyteller app, tap **📺** and **Start sharing**. It shows a 5-letter code. Type the code into the Town Square and it connects; the 📺 button turns blue and says how many screens are connected. Both devices need the internet to find each other (through the free PeerJS service); after that the game goes straight between them. If either side reloads or drops out, they reconnect by themselves, and sharing carries on into the next game. A Town Square open in another window on the same device works without a code.
- **What it shows**, always big enough to read across a room:
  - **Setting up / drawing**: the players, then how many have drawn their character.
  - **Night**: a dark "Night 2: everyone, close your eyes" screen.
  - **Dawn**: the red dawn screen with who was found dead.
  - **📜 Characters**: every character on the script and what it does, grouped by team, with how many of each team there are for this many players. It goes up by itself once everyone has their token, until the first night, and the Storyteller can put it up any time with **📜 Show the characters on the TV** (on the day screen and in the 📺 card); the **Characters** button in the TV's corner does the same. It never shows who has which character. It sizes itself to fit the screen.
- **Day**: the seating circle with names, deaths and ghost votes; the nominator and nominee highlighted while a vote is on, with everyone who has raised a hand lit up and the count against the votes needed; who's on the block; today's nominations and Slayer shots; who was executed. Which part of the day it is with its timer, shown alongside, or full screen when the Storyteller opens it. **📣 Town talk** shows the latest messages players have said to everyone from their phones.
  - **Game over**: who won, then the Grimoire as the Storyteller reveals it. On the Storyteller's game-over screen, tap each player to reveal them on the Town Square, one at a time and in any order: each reveal fills the screen ("Dad was the Poisoner", red for evil, blue for good) before turning over on the circle. **Reveal everyone** turns over the rest, and the reason the game ended appears once everyone is revealed. Tapping a revealed player hides them again.
  - **Private chats**: during the day, who is talking to whom ("💬 Sophie ↔ Coll"), never what they say.
- **Privacy**: the Storyteller app only ever sends public information. Nobody's character leaves the Storyteller's device until the game is over, so there's nothing to find on the Town Square screen.
- **Full screen** and **Change code** are in the corner.

### Private chats between players

Players can link the Player app to the Storyteller's game and text each other privately during the day.

- **Joining**: on the Player app's first screen or in its Grimoire, the **🔗 Join the game** card takes the same code as the Town Square. The app joins you as the player with your name (or asks which one is you). Only one device can be each player: once you've joined as Sophie, nobody else can.
- **Chatting**: during the day, tap **💬 Chat** next to a player. They get a request on their phone to **Accept** or **Decline**. Once accepted, you text each other in a chat window; **Hide** tucks it away (new messages show on the button) and **End chat** finishes it. One chat at a time each.
- **When**: private chats are allowed with anyone during the day's first part, 💬 Private chats. From 🗣️ Public discussion, through 📢 Nominations, until nightfall, players can only chat with their neighbours, the players sitting either side of them; other chats end with a message, and the other 💬 buttons are switched off. At night, players can only chat with their neighbours; other chats end when night falls. The ✋ Nominate button on the phones only appears once nominations are open.
- **📣 Town talk**: during the day, players can also say something to everyone from their phone. It goes up on the Town Square TV, and every phone and the Storyteller see it too. Messages are up to 200 characters, and they're cleared when night falls.
- **Private**: chats are encrypted end to end. The two phones agree a secret key between themselves (ECDH), and every message is scrambled with it (AES-GCM) before it leaves the phone. Messages travel through the Storyteller's device, which passes them on without being able to read them, and doesn't keep them. It knows who is talking to whom (shown on the Town Square and on the 📺 card), never what is said.
- **Dropping out**: a phone that loses its link (a locked screen, a reload) has 30 seconds to come back before its chat ends; when it does, the two phones agree a new key and carry on.
